# Feedback from Ballot CH EMS STU 2

## General information
The STU 2 ballot ([IG v2.0.0-ballot](https://fhir.ch/ig/ch-ems/2.0.0-ballot/index.html)) was held from August 7 to September 30, 2026. The annual Projectathon took place from September 22-23, 2026, with the opportunity to test and provide feedback on the CH EMS exchange format.

Votes cast: 13 (YES: 12, NO: 0, ABSTAIN: 1). There were no negative votes.

During the ballot, 24 issues were reported for CH EMS (see [CH EMS](https://github.com/hl7ch/ch-ems/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20label%3A%22STU%202%20Ballot%22)). One issue concerns CH Core and CH VACD as well; it is listed in [Related issues in CH Core and derived IGs](#related-issues-in-ch-core-and-derived-igs). Here is a brief summary.

Effort estimate:
* **Low**: editorial or a single, local change in FSH, with no discussion needed
* **Medium**: profile change that affects examples or the logical model and its Model Maps, or that needs a decision in the working group or by IVR
* **High**: new design or mapping work, or input from external organizations needed
